Recently IANA became aware of a decision by the manufacturer of our hardware security modules (HSMs) to cease production of the devices. Further, there is no successor product as they are exiting that line of business . The Keyper products we use were in part selected as they were the only viable device that met FIPS 140-2 Level 4 certification, the highest certification possible.
