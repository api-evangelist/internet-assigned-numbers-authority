ICANN org is conducting a study of how the root zone is administered, to “investigate whether there is a need to increase (and if so, how) the robustness of the operational arrangements for making changes to the Root Zone content to reduce or eliminate single points of failure”. The study is being conducted by JAS Advisors. As part of the study, the JAS team will be contacting TLD operators and asking that they complete brief surveys to provide input into the study.
