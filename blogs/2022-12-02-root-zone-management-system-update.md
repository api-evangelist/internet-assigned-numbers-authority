On 13 December 2022 we will be launching the upgrade of the Root Zone Management System (RZMS). To minimize the number of active requests as we transition to the new platform, please submit and approve any change requests by 9 December 2022, 22:00 UTC. Emergency requests will be possible during any periods of downtime while the system is upgraded.
