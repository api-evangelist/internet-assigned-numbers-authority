Our offices will be closed for the holidays from 23 December 2022 to 2 January 2023. The IANA staff will continue to operate on a modified schedule during this time, including processing incoming and pending requests. In light of our Root Zone Management System upgrades, resources will be closely monitoring the system throughout the period.
