A News section is now available on the IANA website, which provides a centralized clearing house for IANA-related news and announcements. Until now, these items have typically been posted in topic-specific mailing lists, web sites, and direct customer communications. The News section is also published as an Atom feed, which can be subscribed to with an RSS reader.
