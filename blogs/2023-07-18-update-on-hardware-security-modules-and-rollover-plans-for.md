In April we announced that the manufacturer of our hardware security modules (HSMs) used to securely store the Root Zone Key Signing Key will cease production of the devices . As noted in that communication, we continued with our previously announced plans to begin the first phases of a KSK rollover. We generated a new KSK at the KSK Ceremony 49 in April, and plan to replicate the KSK to the second facility in the upcoming KSK Ceremony 50 this week.
